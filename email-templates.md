# Meridian — Cold Outreach Templates

Fill in the bracketed placeholders before sending. Keep the subject lines short — under 50 characters performs best in most inboxes. Send from a warmed-up domain (see README) and always send as plain text, not HTML, for the first touch.

## Sending notes

- **Personalize the first line.** A generic opener is the fastest way into spam or the trash — mention the property, development, or agency by name.
- **One ask per email.** Each template ends with a single, low-friction question. Don't stack multiple asks.
- **Warm up your sending domain** for 1–2 weeks (tools like Instantly.ai or Smartlead have free/low-cost warm-up features) before sending volume — this protects deliverability.
- **Follow up twice, not five times.** A short "just bumping this up" after 3–4 days, then one final short note a week later, outperforms long sequences for cold B2B outreach.
