Who we are

About Meridian

Meridian is a single-desk concierge platform for global travel and mobility. Instead of juggling ten different sites, one address covers short-term stays, Airbnb-style rentals, real estate, property management, local tours, flights, and luxury automobiles — with a dedicated local hub in Dhaka.

How it works

We don't hold inventory. Bookings route through trusted affiliate and referral partners, so you always check out on their secure platform. For higher-touch requests — property management, real estate, luxury vehicles — a message goes straight to our desk over WhatsApp or email.

Why "zero-cost"

Meridian is built entirely on free-tier hosting, open affiliate programs, and direct messaging — no paid software required to run it, so the platform can grow before any advertising spend is needed.
